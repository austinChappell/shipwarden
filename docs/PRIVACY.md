# Privacy policy

This policy applies to the ShipWarden app for iPhone and the ShipWarden app for macOS.
Last change: 7 November 2026.

## Summary

ShipWarden does not collect data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.
We do not operate a server for ShipWarden, so your data does not go to us.

## The iPhone app

- The iPhone app connects only to the ShipWarden app on a Mac that you pair with it. The connection is on your
  network or on your VPN. It is encrypted (HTTPS), and the app accepts only the certificate of your Mac.
- The app shows data from your Mac: agent states, terminal screens, worktrees, tasks and pull requests. The app does
  not keep this data after you close it.
- The app keeps the name and the network addresses of the paired Mac on the phone. It keeps the access key of the
  phone in the iOS keychain. "Forget this Mac" deletes them.
- The camera reads only the pairing QR code. The app does not keep images.
- The demo uses sample data and no network.

## The macOS app

- The macOS app keeps its settings and data on your Mac.
- It connects to the services that you set up, for example GitHub and your task manager. It sends them only the
  requests that you start, with your own sign-in. Their privacy policies apply to that data.
- It keeps a hash of the access key of each paired phone. You can remove a phone in Settings › Mobile.
